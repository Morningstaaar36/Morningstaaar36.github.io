Molecules on a quantum computer

Estimates the energy of molecules with a quantum method, and checks every answer against the exact one.

  • Python
  • VQE
  • CUDA-Q
  • IBM Quantum
Details

A molecule's energy changes as its atoms stretch or bend. We did eleven molecules, rebuilt whole curves from a few points, and ran one on a real IBM quantum computer. CSE 402 course project.

What I built: BeH₂ and O₂, including a 110-point map of energy against bond length and angle, the curve fitting, and the IBM run.

SACA-Tox

Predicts whether a molecule is toxic by combining its atom graph with its text description.

  • PyTorch
  • Graph neural network
