AI

Science claim checker

Type a science claim, like "bariatric surgery helps mental health". The program finds the most relevant research papers, an AI reads them, and it answers supported, contradicted or not enough info. It explains why with quotes, and every quote is automatically checked against the paper. Searching 5,183 abstracts takes under a tenth of a millisecond because the search runs on the GPU.

Science

SACA-Tox

Is this molecule toxic? The model looks at it two ways, as a network of atoms and bonds and as a line of text that describes it, and combines both views to decide. On six standard toxicity tests its AUROC score, where 1.0 is perfect, ranges from 0.84 to 0.99.
